# Shared — facts and rules (Option 2.3)

This is the project memory for **Option 2.3, "Everything stops."**, one of three variations of Option 2 (2.2, 2.3 and 2.4). It shares the project's facts with every option. Read it before any work on 2.3 and update it when a fact is confirmed or a rule changes. Mark anything unconfirmed **(TBC)**.

The four memory files work together: `shared.md` (facts and rules), `design.md` (design and motion), `specs.md` (what is done and what is pending) and `writing.md` (how the words are written).

## Rules (always apply)

1. **Each option kept apart.** Option 1 and Option 2 are frozen. Work on this variation happens only in `Maggie website/option-2.3/`. The engine and sources shared by 2.2, 2.3 and 2.4 live in `option-2.x-shared/source/`: a change there reaches all three variations at once.
2. **Brand name:** the brand is registered as **SALON OM**. In text write "Salon OM". In the logo, "SALON" always comes first and stays the small text: tracked above "OM" (stacked) or before it (inline, "SALON │ OM").
3. **Logo:** use only the Option 2 v2 vector files (stacked, inline, OM; ivory, umber or forest). No rings, glows or other marks added to it.
4. **Accent: forest green, one per screen.** Each element chapter may use its own soft accent, only on its number and title full stop. **Never** put several accent colours side by side: no multi-colour strips, no coloured section borders, no separator lines between sections.
5. **Never use the word "home"** in brand copy ("come home", "at home", "feel at home", "home to yourself"). Home is not a welcoming place for everyone. Use words like begin, return to yourself, reconnect, arrive, belong.
6. **Readability first:** text on film or photo always sits on the defined scrim, at 4.5:1 or more on the brightest frame. Body text is never smaller than 16px.
7. **Motion follows design.md → Motion rules.** Every transition eases in and out. Films always loop, in slow motion, and can be paused. Reduced motion makes the choreography gentler; it does not switch it off.
8. **Own media only:** films and photos are generated on the client's Magnific account (or are the client's own). Never use Pinterest or web images. AI people never stand in for the coach.
9. **Real content only:** services, prices, testimonials and contact details stay marked as placeholders until confirmed.
10. **Responsive:** desktop ≥ 1024, tablet 640–1023, phone < 640. Check all three, with no horizontal scroll at 360px.
11. **Memory files:** keep all four current, in the same session as any change: `shared.md` (facts and rules), `design.md` (design and motion), `specs.md` (what is done and pending) and `writing.md` (how the words are written).
12. **Words follow `writing.md`;** motion follows `design.md` → Motion rules.
