# Writing — Option 2.3 "Everything stops."

How the words on this option are written: the voice, the words we use and avoid, the pattern and length of every kind of text on the page, and the checks before anything is published. Read it before writing or changing any copy (headlines, body, buttons, labels, alt text, emails). Update it in the same session when a writing decision changes.

The four memory files work together: `shared.md` (facts and rules), `design.md` (design and motion), `specs.md` (what is done and what is pending) and `writing.md` (how the words are written).

Option 2.3 is about time slowing down. The words are fewer and stiller than in 2.2, and the visit is told on a clock, hour by hour.

## Voice

- Warm, honest and compassionate. Calm, never pushy.
- Speak to one person, as "you".
- Short sentences, one idea each. Plain words over clever ones.
- No hype, urgency, exclamation marks or emoji. No promises of cures or guaranteed outcomes.
- The salon world speaks as "we" and keeps to the present tense, as if time has paused. The coaching world (Clay) is warm and grounded.

## Words

**Use the coach's own vocabulary.** Her words are kept verbatim in `shared.md` → "The coach's own words". Draw on them: heal, grow, transform, reconnect, honesty, compassion, courage, inner wisdom, limiting beliefs, the strength already within you, thrive, aligned, empowered, authentic, believe in yourself again.

**Never "home".** No "come home", "at home", "feel at home", "home to yourself": home is not a welcoming place for everyone (client, 12 Oct 2026). Use begin, begin again, return to yourself, reconnect, arrive, belong.

**Avoid:** hype ("life-changing", "amazing"), urgency ("hurry", "limited", "last chance"), and any promise of a cure or a guaranteed outcome. We support healing and growth; we never claim to fix anyone.

## Rules for the variations

- **The elements stay in the films and the structure, not in the words.** The five films still run, but the copy speaks benefits (what the visit or the coaching gives you). The one exception is the OM line, which names the elements by design: it is the brand's own sentence.
- **Funnel order:** hook → pain or dream → five benefit slides → Aveda → people → offer → bridge to coaching → call to action.
- **Two worlds, one voice.** The salon world speaks as the salon ("we") and names Maggie in the third person ("Held by Maggie."). The coaching world is gentler and more inward, and quotes Maggie's own words.
- **Aveda facts come only from the list in `shared.md`** (founded 1978 by Horst Rechelbacher, "knowledge of the whole", "care for the world we live in", 100% vegan formulas, first beauty company manufacturing with 100% wind power, B Corp, Leaping Bunny). Nothing else about Aveda without a source.
- **People:** the second person in the salon is "[Name]" until the client confirms the name and role. Maggie's portraits stay labelled "Placeholder portrait" until real photos arrive.

## Words on film

- Text on a film is read in a few seconds while the picture moves: one idea, short lines (about 40 characters), never more than three lines of body.
- The big word carries the slide. The body explains it, the specs give the detail. Never repeat the big word in the body.
- On clay slides the line can run longer (about 56 characters) and the copy can breathe a little more.

## Mechanics

- **Sentence case** for headlines, buttons and labels ("Book a session"). Eyebrows and labels are typed in sentence case too; the design sets them in capitals.
- **American spelling:** color, center, program. Maggie's copy direction (Oct 2026) asks for "a California review of every word before launch". Older lines on this page that still say "colour" are corrected when this option's copy is next revised.
- **Brand name:** "Salon OM" in text. Never "Salon Om", "SalonOM" or "OM Salon".
- **Serial comma** in our own copy ("honesty, compassion, and courage"), as in Maggie's own writing. Quotes from the coach stay exactly as she wrote them.
- **Curly quotes and apostrophes** (’ “ ”). Use "&" only in short labels and names ("Soft & strong.", "Cut & Ritual").
- **No exclamation marks, no emoji.**
- **Durations** as "60 min"; prices only once confirmed.
- **Alt text:** one plain sentence saying what is in the picture. Placeholder photos say so ("Placeholder portrait of Maggie, smiling in the salon").

## Placeholders and facts

- Never publish invented testimonials, prices, credentials, addresses or hours. Until they are confirmed, write "Price on request", "Address to confirm", "[Name]" for the second person in the salon, and keep sample quotes labelled as samples.
- Every fact on the page comes from `shared.md` → Facts. Add a new fact there first, with its source, then use it.
- The coach's words are quoted word for word or not at all. Short phrases may be borrowed into our copy; full sentences in quotation marks must match her text exactly.
