# Photography

Placeholder photographs generated with Magnific (Google Nano Banana 2, 2K) on the client's account, saved here as 1600px WebP. The full-resolution originals stay in the Magnific project "Salon OM Website". They set the mood until real photography replaces them. They never stand in for the coach herself.

- `salon-om-coaching.webp`: two linen armchairs, herbal tea and palm shadows in a sunlit room (4:3). The one-to-one coaching card.
- `salon-om-salon.webp`: a calm salon with an arched mirror, a stone basin and dried pampas grass (4:3). The salon / ritual card.
- `salon-om-circle.webp`: linen floor cushions in a circle around a lit candle (4:3). The group / circle card.
- `salon-om-shoreline-walk.webp`: a woman in a linen dress walking the shoreline at golden hour, seen from behind (3:4). The About arch until the coach's portrait arrives.
- `salon-om-sand.webp`, `salon-om-linen.webp`, `salon-om-plaster-palm.webp`: light textures (1:1) for affirmation tiles. Text over them sits on the `photo-veil` wash.

The prompts that produced this look: a warm, desaturated palette of ivory, linen, sand, stone and muted sage; soft natural light; editorial interior photography; no people, text or logos.
