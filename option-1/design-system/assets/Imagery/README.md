# Imagery

Textures generated for Salon OM from noise and geometry. No photographs or third-party images, so there are no licensing questions. Use them on cards, affirmation tiles, arches and mood strips. Replace them with real photography where people expect to see a real thing (the coach's portrait, the space).

- `sand-ripples.webp`: wind-shaped ripples in pale sand under raking light (1600×1200). Card and tile backgrounds.
- `plaster-palm.webp`: warm ivory plaster with a slant of sun and palm-frond shadows (1200×1500, portrait). Arches, About.
- `sage-frond.webp`: sage-washed plaster with a soft frond shadow (1200×900). The one sage moment on a page.
- `linen.webp`: a fine linen weave (1200×1200). Affirmation tiles, quiet panels.
- `stone.webp`: pale travertine with soft layers and pores (1200×1200). Cards, tiles.
- `shore-still.webp`: one frame of the `Shoreline` sea (960×1200). Its fallback when WebGL is unavailable, and for social share images.

All are light and low-contrast, so `ink` text reads on them. Check each pairing before setting text over an image.
