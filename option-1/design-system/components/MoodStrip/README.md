# MoodStrip

The palette at its real proportions: one strip whose bands are as wide as each colour's share of a page, with tiers and a legend underneath.

- **Provide:** nothing, to show the house mix (Neutrals 85% · Naturals 9% · Accents 6%). Or pass `bands` (`{name, token, weight, tier, image}[]`), `height`, `legend: false`, `labels: false`.
- Read it as a rule: about 85% of any page is ivory, linen, sand and stone. Sage and ink make up about 9%. Gold, forest, ocean, clay and chestnut together are about 6%, for accents only.
- With `image` on a band, the strip becomes a texture board (use the Imagery group). Widths still follow `weight`.
- Labels hide inside narrow bands and on mobile. The legend always carries every name and share.
