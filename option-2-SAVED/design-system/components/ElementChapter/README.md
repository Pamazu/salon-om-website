# ElementChapter

One of the five element slides: a full-screen slow-motion film with bottom-left copy. The element's soft accent appears **only** on the chapter number and the title's full stop.

- **Provide:** `element` (`earth` | `water` | `fire` | `air` | `ether`), `index`, `name`, `sanskrit`, `title` (one word, the full stop is added), `body` (one or two sentences), `specs` (three label/value pairs), `video`, `poster`.
- Entrances: meta from the left (0.2s), the word out of its mask (0.35s), body (0.75s), specs (0.95 / 1.07 / 1.19s); the film settles from big to normal over 3s.
- Phones show two specs; screens under 700px tall hide them.
- Films: Ground (driftwood light), Water (shoreline), Fire (candles), Air (curtains), Ether (smoke). Never sand for Ground.
