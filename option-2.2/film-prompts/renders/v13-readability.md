# v13 blind readability (claude -p, Read only, frames 1 + 120 of the 5 s, no context)

Both frames show the same shot. The camera pushes in a little and drifts slightly left. Nothing else moves: no arms, no head, and the water looks the same.

**1) Where and what's happening**
It reads as a hair-wash basin, like the ones in a salon. A person lies back with their head in a black basin and a tall faucet behind it. A large grey figure stands over them at the right and reaches down toward the head with both arms. Without the title, I'd guess "someone getting their hair washed," but only from the basin and the faucet. Nothing in the frame confirms it.

**2) Who's in it, and their hands**
- **Lying person:** skin-toned, with no detail. You can't see their hands. The large beige cylinder in the foreground could be the neck or a raised arm, and the large mass at the bottom right could be the chest or a shoulder. I can't tell which.
- **Standing figure:** plain grey with no texture, and its head is out of frame. Both arms reach down to the lying person's head: one to the top near the faucet, one to the side of the face. **No hands or fingers are modelled.** The arms just end behind the head, so you can't tell whether it's washing, holding or pressing.
- On the left of the head, a **grey crescent** sticks out of the yellow. It's the same grey as the standing figure. It could be a hand, a clip, a horn or a claw; I can't tell.

**3) Water**
A clear vertical column runs from the spout straight down onto the top of the yellow mass, with a little white foam where it lands. It's the same see-through material as the faucet, so it reads more like a **glass rod** than running water. You can't see it go anywhere afterwards: there's no water in the basin, no runoff, no wet hair and no drain.

**4) The wall behind**
- A light grey shelf with two rails holds five very tall coloured cylinders: dark red, raspberry, green, pale see-through blue and grey-green. They read more like giant test tubes or pillars than product bottles, and they run off the top of the frame.
- Behind the grey figure is a dark brown rectangle in a black frame. It could be a window, a door or a picture.
- A tall brown slab fills the left edge of the frame (about a sixth of it). It could be a door or the side of a cabinet, and it seems to have no purpose in the shot.

**5) Flat or confusing**
Nothing looks like a printed photo. Everything is plain, flat-shaded clay. The brown rectangle is the flattest-looking part and reads like a painted board. What's confusing:
- **The face reads as a teddy bear.** The round bump on top looks like a bear's ear, with one brown eye mark and a pink blush or mouth mark. The bump is probably the nose seen from below the chin.
- **The yellow mass** doesn't read as hair. It looks like a lumpy cushion or towel lying in the basin.
- The lying body can't be read as a body (see point 2).
- The water looks solid (see point 3).

**6) Creepy or unsettling**
Yes. A huge, faceless, headless grey figure looms over a person lying back with their neck stretched. Its arms, which have no hands, reach to the face and neck while water pours onto the head. The grey crescent looks like a claw. Cold, it reads as **someone being held down under running water** more than as a wash. The size difference makes it worse: the grey figure's torso fills the right third of the frame.

## Designer 1 reading
- Hands seem not to move: the 1 cm circle takes 5 s per turn, so frames 1 and 120 show the same pose; they move in between.
- Water reads as a glass rod: fixed in the next pass (whiter, less see-through, streaks that move).
- Teddy-bear face, tall bottles cropped at the top, the grey crescent (the stylist's near hand): the same as v12, which take 5 scored RIGHT.
