Why Aveda

Beauty from the inside out.

Salon OM is an Aveda Concept Salon. Aveda was founded in 1978 by Horst Rechelbacher to create holistic beauty through wellness. Its name means “knowledge of the whole,” which is what OM means to us.

Guests often come for the scents, the rituals, and the results, and stay for what Aveda stands for.
