What is in the work?

Grounded in research. Space to Grow. Our evidence-informed work draws on coaching, hypnotherapy, Rapid Transformational Therapy®, somatic practices, IFS-informed parts work and neuroscience.

Coaching
gives you clear goals, practical tools and support to put new insights into action.
Hypnotherapy
