Lenticular sheet, four options

A clear ribbed sheet like the one in the video, held over the Why Aveda photo. Each one moves by itself; scroll and move your pointer over it too (on a phone, scroll or drag). Tell me a letter, or mix two: "C's sheet with A's reveal".

AThe sheet is lowered. About twelve wide lenses, held well away from the photo, so each band stretches its strip into soft vertical streaks. The sheet lowers and rises by itself, its clean top edge showing the sharp photo above; scrolling lowers it further.
BThe sheet breathes. Very fine ribs over the whole photo, milky like the sheet in the video. It drifts closer to the photo and away again by itself, so the shelves clear and dissolve into streaks; a rainbow travels across it.
CHalf a sheet, sliding. A thick sheet of medium lenses covers only the words' half and slides sideways by itself, so every band redraws its piece of the shelves; where it ends you see its glass edge, a rainbow fringe and the sharp photo. Scrolling slides it too.
DA sheet in the hand. A smaller sheet of four big lenses that flip and squeeze what is behind them. It drifts by itself and follows your pointer (on a phone, drag it); the words ride on it, the photo around it stays sharp.
