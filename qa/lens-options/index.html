Lenticular sheet, four options

The clear ribbed sheet from the video, over the Why Aveda photo. Each one is live: scroll the page and move your pointer over it (on a phone, scroll). Tell me a letter, or mix: "B's lenses with A's reveal".

AThe sheet slides away. Eight wide lenses, strong sideways smear, a rainbow fringe. Scroll down: the sheet slides off to the right and the sharp photo is revealed, the words travel with it.
BThe sheet stays. Seven wide lenses over the whole photo, never clear: the bands shift and re-smear as you scroll or move the pointer; words on the sheet's pale side.
CHalf a sheet. Five lenses cover only the words' half; where the sheet ends the photo is sharp, with a soft rainbow edge. The edge follows your pointer a little; scroll slides the lenses.
DA sheet you move. A floating sheet of five lenses follows your pointer (on a phone, it travels with the scroll); the faster you move it the more it smears; the words ride on it; everywhere else the photo is sharp.
