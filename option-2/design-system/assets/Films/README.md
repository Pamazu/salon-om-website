# Films

The Option 2 films and their posters, generated on the client's Magnific account and encoded as **slow-motion seamless loops**: slowed to 0.5x (elements) or 0.6x (camera moves) with motion-interpolated frames at 30fps, H.264 High, no audio, faststart. Each film has a WebP poster of its first frame.

- `film-hero-rainforest.mp4`: a slow push through a misty rainforest with light shafts (Seedance 2.5). Hero and closing film. Crossfade loop, 13.6s.
- `film-earth-ground-wood.mp4`: driftwood grain under a sweep of golden light (Seedance 2.5). Ground. Plays forward then back, 16.5s.
- `film-water.mp4`: turquoise water over sand, from above (Kling 2.5). To be revisited with the client's beach reference.
- `film-fire.mp4`: candle flames and embers in darkness (Kling 2.5).
- `film-air.mp4`: linen curtains breathing in sunlight (Kling 2.5). The brightest film: check the scrim.
- `film-ether.mp4`: incense smoke through a beam of light (Kling 2.5).

Element films loop with a 2.5s crossfade from their end into their start (17.4s).
