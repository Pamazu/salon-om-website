# OfferTile

A photo tile for an offering, with copy on a bottom gradient and two actions.

- **Provide:** `image` + `alt`, `tag` (type and duration), `title`, `text`, `primary` and `secondary` links.
- Place three in `.tiles` inside an `.offers` slide. On arrival the tiles open upward one after another (1.7s, 0.15s apart) while their photos settle from 132% to 104%. Below 1024px the row becomes a horizontal swipe carousel.
- Primary buttons are forest for every tile (one accent).
